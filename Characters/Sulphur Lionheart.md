# Sulphur Lionheart — Character Concept

> [!info] Character Snapshot
> - **Name:** Sulphur Lionheart *(Alternate / alias: **Sul**, pronounced "Sol", used by mentors who respect him. It nods to the Latin for sun, a link to [[Weld]]. "Sulphur" also means brimstone, which suits his Vengeance oath.)*
> - **Homeland:** [[Flavo Topis]] *(serving 20 years in Ochre, [[Cirranbar]])*
> - **Patron Deity:** [[Weld]] *(sun, purity, justice)*
> - **Starting Level:** 3 *(age 45)*
> - **Class Concept:** Human Paladin (Oath of Vengeance). A glaive-and-spear enforcer whose vengeance has curdled into execution. Weapon Mastery (Graze, Sap) and Vow of Enmity are his "remove the problem" toolkit.
> - **Elevator Pitch:** A burnt-out Flavic paladin who delivers justice with a glaive, but whose faded optimism is the only thing that can still save him.

---

## Character Concept: The Burnt-Out Lion
Sulphur was a child of a wandering clan until a templar knight noticed his gift for the divine and his stubborn sense of justice. He trained at the top holy order in Marigold, graduated 6th, and was tricked out of his prestigious posting by a scheming noble family. Twenty years stuck in the bandit-ruled town of Ochre have left him a drunk and a chain-smoker with tattered armor and one remaining talent: dealing with bad guys. The Chromaclasm quest in Harlequin is his first chance to leave, and he has to find himself again before he can help the realm.

### Personality & Quirks
- **Core Trait:** Gallows-dry humor.
- **Core Trait:** Brutally efficient in a fight, yet he still does small kindnesses when he thinks no one is watching.
- **Core Trait:** Allergic to paperwork, which is a bad fit for [[Maradosa]].
- **Signature Habit / Catchphrase:** Lights a cigarette before every fight and mutters the first line of his oath.

### Flaws, Fears & Bonds
- **Flaw:** Drinks to numb himself, and no longer offers mercy or a second chance.
- **Fear / Weakness:** That the optimism is gone for good, and that he has become the kind of man he swore to stop.
- **Bond:** His dead partner, **Aurelia Amberward**, the other paladin of Ochre, who died 15 years ago. Her order emblem is the one thing he keeps clean. *(Player-invented NPC. Who she was to him is still undecided.)*
- **Ideal:** Justice is only worth enforcing if it protects someone.

### Appearance & Voice
- **Look:** Broad and heavy-shouldered, gone slightly soft from drink. Grey-shot stubble, a Flavic sun-weathered tan, and deep-set tired eyes. His armor is faded to the colour of old mustard, dented, with a tattered tabard and mismatched straps.
- **Voice / Mannerisms:** Low, gravelly and unhurried. Short, flat sentences, and he coughs when he laughs. Rolls a cigarette when he's thinking, and goes quiet before he gets violent.
- **Gear with a Story:** Aurelia's order emblem (on his sheet). The glaive, the one thing he still oils and maintains. Armor and tabard worn down by twenty years in Ochre, where each damaged piece can stand for a broken tenet.

## Backstory Outline (Level 3)
- **Origins:** Born into a wandering clan that passed through [[Flavo Topis]]. A templar knight noticed his natural connection to the gods and his resilient, just character, and sponsored him into the order's training and scholarship.
- **Defining Event:** He graduated 6th in the top 10, which earned him the honor of a pilgrimage in the capital, Marigold. The son of a noble lord had been pushed into that place by bribes and favors, and lost to Sulphur. He was then convinced and tricked into giving up his spot to serve "the people who needed it most" in Ochre. There is secret corruption at the top of the order, and Sulphur unintentionally undermined other nobles' and priests' schemes.
- **Why They Left / Why They Adventure:** Ochre has been the hell-hole of his life. House Gildenmead pulled strings to deny every transfer request. The announced quest in Harlequin related to the Chromaclasm is his first real chance to leave his post and help the realm.
- **Experience So Far:** Twenty years in Ochre. The only other paladin stationed there died 15 years ago. The local police are paid off, so he keeps crime down by removing the problem for good, and has long since stopped trying to reform bandits.
- **The Hook:** The Chromaclasm quest announced in Harlequin ([[Maradosa]]), see [[Campaign Pitch]] and [[The Chromaclasm]].

### The Nemesis: House Gildenmead *(player-invented, not canon)*
Minor Flavic nobles who bought their rank with tithes and quiet favors. The son, **Fennimore "Fenn" Gildenmead**, is a soft, sulking schemer who could not win a capital posting on merit, so his family bribed and tricked Sulphur out of his. They keep Sulphur posted in Ochre through paid clerks and "lost" transfer paperwork. Cowardly, scheming, and quietly hoping Sulphur will cross a line.

## Sulphur's Oaths *(player-invented, for roleplay)*

### 1. The Graduate's Vow (age ~25, Marigold)
Sworn to [[Weld]]. The oath he is proudest of and most ashamed of.
- *Protect the people who can't protect themselves.*
- *Serve the crown's justice, not any one lord's.*
- *Offer reform before the blade.*

Twenty years in Ochre have broken the third line, and that hurts most. The first line is the reason he was tricked into taking the posting.

### 2. The Ochre Oath (age ~30, at his dead partner's grave)
The oath that makes him a Vengeance paladin. Sworn in grief, with no priest or ceremony. It is his own, not tied to Weld.
- **No mercy for the wicked.** If the law won't act, he will, permanently.
- **Remove the problem.** Bandits are cut off at the root, not warned and released.
- **The dead are owed.** Whoever kills the innocent answers for it, whatever it costs him.

This is the oath he lives by now and the root of his Vow of Enmity. It is also a trap: each tenet can be read as a license to kill anyone who annoys him, and the Gildenmeads are hoping he'll cross that line.

### 3. The Unsworn Oath (waiting to be spoken)
Where his arc is heading (**Recommit**). He keeps Vengeance but adds mercy and restitution, and it should only be spoken at a moment the table earns.
- **Restitution.** Repair what his failures allowed to happen.
- **Fight the cause, not just the symptom.** The corruption at the top, not only the bandits.
- **Mercy is a choice, not a weakness.** His old optimism, returned harder and wiser.

### At the table
- Every time he kills when mercy was possible, he is fine under oath 2 and failing oath 1.
- Oathbreaker risk should be a *threat in the story* rather than a mechanical penalty, unless the DM wants otherwise (Session 0 question).
