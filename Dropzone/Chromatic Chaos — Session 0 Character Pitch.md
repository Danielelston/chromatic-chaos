## Character Concept: The Unfazed Diner Cook
Bramwell Ironroast is a laid-back, fiercely competitive, endlessly curious chef who grew up working a humble neighborhood diner in Calamansi. Where the city's elite culinary dynasties obsess over rigid tradition and high-society status, Bram treats cooking like a street fight: he uses cheap, weird, or salvaged ingredients, adapts to any disaster on the fly, and refuses to back down from a challenge—no matter how outmatched he looks.

### Personality & Quirks
- **Unshakeable Confidence:** Never panics when a plan goes sideways. If a monster ambushes the camp or an ingredient spoils, he just grins, ties his headband tighter, and treats it as a fun problem to solve.
- **Relentless Experimenter:** Constantly tests bizarre, borderline-inedible flavor combinations (like honey-pickled bog-beetle or salted-caramel viper jerky) and insists the party act as his taste-testers.
- **Zero Respect for Snobbery:** Completely unfazed by pompous nobles, snooty rival chefs, or intimidating warlords. He judges everyone strictly by their work ethic and whether they finish their plate.
- **Signature Habit:** Wraps a stained cloth headband around his forehead whenever things get serious—whether he is cooking a high-stakes meal or stepping into initiative—and announces, *"Order up!"* when finishing a fight or a dish.

## Backstory Outline (Level 3)
- **The Family Diner:** Bram grew up working the kitchen of *Ironroast's Corner*, a cramped, noisy, no-frills diner tucked right on the border dividing Calamansi's two bitterly feuding culinary elites—the Sweet Dynasty and the Savory Syndicate. While the elite families waged a pretentious cold war over high-end gastronomy, Bram's family fed dockworkers, farmers, and travelers whatever was fresh, cheap, and filling.
- **The Calabash Upset:** During last year's [[The Calabash|Calabash]] festival on [[Lake Limon]], Bram entered on a whim to promote his family's diner. Using humble street-food techniques, he smashed together Sweet and Savory traditions into a single, ridiculously good dish (a glazed, spice-crusted roast served with sweet-tart reduction). The crowd loved it, the aristocratic judges were horrified by the "low-born heresy," and the elite families had him disqualified on a technicality to protect their reputations.
- **Hitting the Road:** Rather than sulk—or let the rival families bully his neighborhood—Bram decided he needed to learn every cooking style in [[Akroma]] so nobody could ever dismiss him on a technicality again. He packed his battered skillet, tied on his headband, and set out to test himself against the rest of the continent.
- **Earning His Stripes (Levels 1–3):** Over the last two years, Bram worked as a mercenary camp cook and forward forager along the rough borderlands near the [[Chartreuse Mire]] and [[Cirranbar]]. He learned how to harvest volatile glands and medicinal herbs from dangerous monsters, fought off dune raiders and bog-beasts with a skillet and alchemical fire, and built a reputation as an unflappable guy who can whip up a five-star meal—or a life-saving tonic—out of whatever happens to be lying around.
- **The Seppheros Contract:** After breezing through [[Maradosa]]'s endless licensing paperwork with sheer stubbornness to get his adventuring permit stamped, Bram took the [[Seppheros]] relic job. The payout will keep his family's diner thriving, and he is dying to find out what kind of forgotten pre-Chromaclasm ingredients might be hiding in the northern ruins.

## Character Goals

### Short-Term Goals (First Few Sessions)
- [ ] **Conquer the Seppheros Kitchen:** Recover the client's relic from the ruins of Seppheros while hunting down weird, mutated plants or monster ingredients to test new recipes on the party.
- [ ] **Win Every Campfire Challenge:** Establish himself as the party's undisputed cook, turning even the worst dungeon rations into morale-boosting meals.
- [ ] **Field-Test Weird Combos:** Subject the party to at least three experimental "fusion snacks" before they figure out how to dodge taste-test duty.

### Medium-Term Goals (Mid-Campaign)
- [ ] **The Nine-Nation Cooking Duel:** Challenge local chefs, tavern keepers, and masters in every nation of Akroma to friendly cook-offs, stealing their best techniques—from [[Argrenath]] volcanic-stone searing to [[Methysto]] crystal-infused reductions.
- [ ] **Impress Quercitron:** Honor the harvest god's belief that good food should never be overpriced or elitist, hoping to earn a nod from one of his seed-carrying ankylosaurs.
- [ ] **Master the "Akroma Menu":** Develop an original nine-course menu that incorporates signature ingredients from all nine nations without relying on noble snobbery.

### Long-Term Goals (Endgame)
- [ ] **Take the Calabash Crown:** Return to Calamansi, enter The Calabash on Lake Limon as a seasoned adventurer-chef, and beat both the Sweet and Savory elites so decisively that neither family can disqualify him.
- [ ] **Make *Ironroast's Corner* Legendary:** Use his adventuring fortune to turn his humble neighborhood diner into the most famous cross-cultural eatery in Akroma, where anyone from a Mint Mogul to a [[Mudruckers|Mudrucker]] can grab a cheap, unforgettable meal.
