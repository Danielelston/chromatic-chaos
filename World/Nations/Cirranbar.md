# Cirranbar — The Scorched Dunelands

*Pronounced:* Sear-anne-bar; Adj. Cirr or Cirran

## Summary
Freelands and frontiers. The lands were long disputed by both Argrenath and Flavo Topis. After a ceasefire, it was agreed to be an intermittent territory. Considered lawless and savage wilds by many. Several bandits and raiders have made this country their homes, and the flora and fauna are twice as vicious. One misstep can be the end of even the toughest of adventurers.

## Culture
After years of battle and strife staining the land, most inhabitants have turned against civilization and ruling leaders. The desert lands are harsh and take no survivors; those who live here have hardened their bodies and their hearts. People have adapted to more nomadic lifestyles as settlements tend not to last long in the waving dunes. There are also those who have a sense of adventure in their hearts, and travel the lands in search of a promised land.

## Government
The closest thing to a ruling force here are the bandit gangs and rebel groups who take lands by force.

## Environment
The land is a long dry desert with sweeping sandstorms. Parts to the north bordering the Coral Peaks have had more flourishing climates, even able to host crops. Despite the arid climate, large and hardy creatures have managed to sustain themselves, including dinosaurs.

## Settlements
### Apricot
A lively land of farmers and mercenaries who dedicate themselves to living life off of their own earnings, completely isolated from international affairs. The people here are extremely wary of outsiders, and only loose contact from small settlements like Moccasin have made any contact.

### Ochre
A host of scoundrels and ruled by bandits. It’s ill-advised to enter this area without a hardy entourage. The Dune King, leader of the Ochre Bandits, has made his residence here.

### Oriole (Oar-ee-ohl)
A courier jointly governed by Argrenath and Flavo Topis. It’s more or less a post for militaries of both parties. It hosts the only functional teleporter for the three countries.

## Other Places
### Coral Peaks
A large mountain range to the north that separates the country from the Feldgrau Forest. Various bandits are said to reside in this area.

### Persimmon Ruins (Purr-sih-mahn)
A collection of old temples left behind by the old ruler Persimmon, the Rune King. There are said to be labyrinthine catacombs beneath with ancient secrets.

### Sunset Valley
Many come in search of a promised land, but none have succeeded and come back to tell the tale. It’s rumored to be the center of a great paradise where Saffron resides.
