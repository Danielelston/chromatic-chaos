# Flavo Topis — The Sun-Touched Kingdom

*Pronounced:* Flahv-oh Toh-pis; Adj. Flav or Flavic

| | |
|---|---|
| **Capital** | Marigold |
| **Races** | Humans, halflings |
| **Political** | King Xanther dul Flavo XIV |
| **Commerce** | Crops, Produce, Tourism |
| **Deities** | [[Fustic]], [[Quercitron]], [[Myrabalan]] |

## Summary
A picturesque and sunny country; Flavo Topis is home to many tourist spots within Akroma. The country prides itself in its bountiful farmlands, rolling green hills, and shimmering beaches. The people are humble, earnest, and jovial. They love and welcome people like neighbors, and have a friendly disposition.

## Culture
Raised on the laurels of Weld, the people here are pleasant and pious. The Flavs are much more forward about their beliefs, which in the past spurred crusades. After the peace treaty with Argrenath, the current King had decided to settle down the tempers of the people. They are however quite quaint in their views, shying away from the arcane and mechanical advancements of other countries. A life here can be peaceful, if a bit mundane.

## Government
A ceremony of succession in the name of Fustic is held to name the new King. It is by his holy authority that the will of Weld be done. The king’s rule is absolute, and to question him is to question the gods themselves. The king also has direct authority over the Aurum Gauntlet and the most trusted become part of the Knights of Sun, his loyal protectors. The king as of recent has directed most of his efforts towards making Flavo Topis a tourist paradise as a show of good faith and to help with reparations after the war.

## Organizations
The Aurum Gauntlet is a large order of paladins headquartered in Aureolin. They seek to purge the world of all monsters and bring an era of prosperity for the Flavs. Many were crusaders during the Blazing Sun War. There’s also the council of Fustic; advisers of the king and the prophets who help decide new ordinances and, when the time comes, rulers.

## Environment
The land is much more temperate than the other southern countries. Flavo Topis enjoys the privilege of a vastly diverse ecosystem of vibrant flora and gentle fauna. It’s because of this that the area has been such a popular tourism area. It is mostly plains and hills. A lot of the area has been converted to farms and towns, with a few forests spared for their prestige, like the wild Jean-Quille Orchard which hosts golden-leaf trees during harvest season.

## Settlements
### Aureolin (Oar-ee-oh-linne)
A garrison for the Aurum Gauntlet. Those who seek to become champions of the gods typically enlist themselves here. They’re also the country's major producer of silver.

### Begonia
The floral crest of Flavo Topis. The city is more for a large garden host to many interesting plants and animals. The royal family tend to spend long vacations here.

### Calamansi
A gourmand city. It has a heated rivalry of two cooking families on which flavor was better: sweet or savory. It split the city in two, and has since created a lively competition every harvest year called the Calabash at the center of Lake Limon.

### Canary
A courier town. Since travel is so important to the Flavs, this town is constantly bustling with activity. There are fancy ships here that go across the Flavesce Bay to Begonia.

### Flaxen
Known as the town of new beginnings, ex-convicts, retired soldiers, and those down on their luck come here to cleanse their souls of sin and start anew. Despite what most would assume, it is a diligent and quiet town.

### Maize
The major farming city. The land is especially fertile, to which they thank Quercitron. being a major export of food, and at the center of Flavo Topis, they’ve also become a center of transportation within the country.

### Marigold
A beautiful city surrounding Castle Fulvos. In wanting to make this city as beautiful as possible, the king has enlisted several famous artists and cultural savants to live here. It’s mostly known as the city of Renaissance, and to be accepted here is one of the highest honors.

### Moccasin
A town that was once a part of Cirranbar. Despite the truce between nations, the land is still considered claimed by the Flavs. Due to being so far from the kingdom, they haven’t been given the same luxury treatment as other major towns, and have had to rely on Apricot for help.

## Other Places
### Buff Bay
A gorgeous bay where most come to cruise. It’s also heavily defended by Flav ocean liners. The beaches are considered one of the most beautiful places in Akroma.

### Chartreuse Mire
A muggy swamp that goes along the border between Flavo Topis and Cirranbar. In the Flav’s first attempts to expand westward, they settled the lands claimed from the latter. Witches however are said to have cursed the land for their hubris, turning the land into a dangerous bog.

### Jean-Quille Orchard (John-Kweel)
A massive orchard in the eastern part of Flavo Topis. It was started by the man Jean-Quille long ago who wanted to create food for his people. He was said to be blessed by Weld, and reigned as the king of Flavo Topis after his deeds.

### Lake Limon (Lee-mohn)
A large lake near Calamansi. It’s where the Calabash is held, a grand event where chefs of the city compete to see who can make the best food in the country. Connoisseurs from all over the continent come to test dishes.
