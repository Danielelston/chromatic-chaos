# Covalt — The Boreal Peninsula

*Pronounced:* Kuh-vault; Adj. Covalti

| | |
|---|---|
| **Capital** | Capri Towers |
| **Races** | Elves, aquatic races, kobolds; dragonborns (rare) |
| **Political** | Cerulean Council |
| **Commerce** | Sailing, magic, education |
| **Deities** | [[Anil]], [[Lazuli]], [[Logwood]] |

## Summary
A country of magical scholars and sailors. It’s freeform in its faculty and law. Most people come here in search of knowledge or to sail the ocean. Their ties with Anil makes them the safest to sail with. Being so close and so curious, they’ve invested heavily in the secrets of Seppheros, particularly in dragons. This has led to a rather interesting development of draconic creatures that have since spread in popularity among the Covalti.

## Culture
The people of Covalt are more bookish and explorative. They are constantly seeking to learn more, particularly about ancient and lost history. The people of Covalt are not swayed by morals and only seek to gain and know more. Their fascination with Seppheros and the arcane has also led them to try and recreate dragons. Though unsuccessful, they ended up creating creatures known as Kobolds, which became a huge trend as adorable little servants across the north.

## Government
A group of scholars curious about the mysteries of Akroma created the Capri Towers as a consortium of sorts. Those founding scholars and other like-minded individuals have gained authority through their merits, and overtime pick an apprentice from one of their schools to be the next head. As time went by, they found themselves advising national, and then international issues, and soon became the political heads and representatives of Covalt.

## Organizations
The curiosity of magic has driven the funding and authority of Covalt. Each major city has specialized in a school of magic, and a physical school devoted to it, with their own approach to uncovering the truth of Akroma. The headmasters of each school also function as a chair at the Cerulean Council. They mostly discuss political functions and recent discoveries. There are also several bands of pirates who raid the coasts of Covalt and the Brackish Ocean

## Environment
Further north than any other country, Covalt is known for its extremely cold climate. In the northern peninsula, it’s a snowy tundra. The strait near the islands tends to freeze completely during the winter, making it difficult to traverse from the mainland to Azure Island and the Capri Towers.

## Settlements
### Capri Towers
A citadel where scholars gather to share knowledge and discuss any future endeavors for the magic schools. The schools here are more invested in the new and unlearned aspects of magic, and are obsessed with experimentation.

### Cornflower
Also called the city of bones, Cornflower is much more secluded than the other major cities. Their pursuit is more archaeological, studying bones of old creatures to see how they might have lived. Cornflower uses necromancy for a more “hands-on” approach to the field.

### Jay
The courier town. It’s also the major of conjuration magic. They have recently discovered advanced forms of teleportation and have spread the invention to other couriers for better convenience.

### Denim
One of the two port cities of Covalt. Denim handles international travel along The Bleakened Ocean. They specialize in abjuration, and have had their students travel the continent to aid others against magical threats.

### Glaucous
The other port city of Covalt. It’s mainly responsible for traveling to and from the mainland. They also specialize in transmutation, and are responsible for the creation of kobolds. They’ve also developed ways to artificially create draconic humanoids, however the details are a bit drastic.

### Periwinkle
A small gnome village far to the north that specializes in illusion magic. It is surrounded by strange trees called Periwillows. These trees make it impossible to locate the town without a guide.

### Smalt
A small town focused primarily on enchantment. This also includes enchanted items, and is said to have a large collection of magical items both made and discovered.

## Other Places
### Azure Fjord
A frigid flow of water between the island of Azure and mainland Covalt

### Mt. Celeste
The tallest mountain in Akroma, it is also called the Torch’s Peak. Its height ensures that the stars are always in view, and as such is chosen as the major of divination magic.

### Zaffre Isles
An archipelago along the Azure Fjord. In order to deal with the rampant issues of pirates and ice floes around the area, they’ve elected to specialize in evocation magic. The school there is said to be much more chaotic than others.
