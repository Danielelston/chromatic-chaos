# Maradosa — The Verdant Glades

*Pronounced:* Mare-uh-doh-suh; Adj. Maradosan

| | |
|---|---|
| **Capital** | Shamrock |
| **Races** | Gnomes & Halflings; Elves and fey (Feldgrau) |
| **Political** | Mint Moguls |
| **Commerce** | Coin, forestry, magitek |
| **Deities** | [[Broom]], [[Verdigris]] |

## Summary
A guild empire; affluent and profiteering. They hold a monopoly on the buying and selling of commonly-produced magical wares. There is also a large forest in the west that is known to host several druid circles and other tribes.

## Culture
Because of the inhabitants being mostly halflings and gnomes, Maradosa has been defined by a very eccentric and colorful lifestyle. The people are always looking for something new and big, and many try their hands at making the next big thing. The guilds like to see themselves as free-thinking opportunists, though some would simply call them money-grubbing. The west however is said to be filled with troublesome folk who attack nearby villages.

## Government
The metropolis of Maradosa is basically one giant conglomerate. Each of the guilds can put a number of stock and equity into the country to gain influence and land. Those in the upper echelons are known as Mint Moguls. Guilds hold several proceedings to determine stock and trade values. The Feldgrau Forest however is beyond their influence. Rather, it is ungoverned but watched over by many druidic circles, each called a Branch of the Great Oak. The Olive Branch are the most amicable and made open paths for trusted caravans throughout the forest.

## Organizations
The Citrine Eye is the headquarters of the guilds and where stock and values are watched heavily. There is a conglomerate of other guilds dispersed around Maradosa that control major functions. Other major guilds include the Crocco Gang who handle “protection” in Peridot, the Jade Sails who handle sailing in Mindaro, and Teal Steelworks which facilitates a lot of the inventions in Malach Heights. There’s also the Celadon Cards, who runs the casinos in Harlequin and received a recent boom in influence.

## Environment
The area to the north is much more verdant and vibrant, with a vast forest taking up much of the country. The Feldgrau Forest is vast with untamed life, while the east has many windy grasslands and a veritable metropolis. There is a heavy fey influence allowing sprawling flora and odd fauna even within the more industrious east.

## Settlements
### Harlequin
Bordering Methysto has influenced this city quite a bit. It’s themed towards flashy spectacles, heavy gambling, and drinking. Several casinos and tourist traps have been set up for the less savvy spenders.

### Kakapo (Caw-kuh-poh)
The courier city. The city keeps records of guild activity and international shipments. The biggest collection of pencil pushers there ever was. Documentation is a major trade here, and it’s where all paperwork is processed.

### Laurel (Yanny)
A druid village deep within the Feldgrau Forest. The Olive Branch of the Great Oak has allowed travellers through this area in for passage to other countries in the north. It stands as the public center for the druids, though some dislike the welcoming of outsiders.

### Malach Heights
A town in the business of invention. There people here are all looking to make the next big thing. A paradise for the creative tinkering types, with steam and machinery as far as the eye can see. They also specialize in fabricating mithril, a light yet durable material great for machinery.

### Mindaro (Min-dahr-oh)
On an island in the east. It’s focused more on shipping and vessels. They also have an appreciation for the fine arts and are shrewd connoisseurs of worldly historic pieces.

### Peridot
A small village closest to the Feldgrau Forest. It mostly houses some mercenary guilds who can be a bit rough to newcomers. The mercenaries are mainly to deal with Darkspring inhabitants sabotaging foresting guilds

### Shamrock
Home of the Citrine Eye, the HQ in Maradosa. Those looking to make a name for themselves or come here to find a guild or start their own. People watch closely the flow of trade to determine their standings amongst the guilds.

## Other Places
### Feldgrau Forest
A giant forest that takes up most of Maradosa. It’s teeming with strange and wild creatures as well as druids. The most central tribes tend to be very secretive and keep to themselves. The other tribes, however, have made efforts in keeping others off their forests.

### Lake Veridian
A large lake near Laurel. The druid circles use the waters for healing and mystic cleansing, and rivers flow from it in all directions. There is a massive oak tree called the Great Oak in the center of the lake that only head druids may visit.

### Sea of Konbu
A sea between Maradosa and Flavo Topis. It’s the main method of travel in the west. Mindaro, however, controls most sailing in the area, and depending on the time of season it can get quite expensive.
