# Methysto — The Midnight Jubilee

*Pronounced:* Meh-this-toh; Adj. Methystan

| | |
|---|---|
| **Capital** | Wisteria |
| **Races** | Drow; magical creatures and outsiders |
| **Political** | Eminence Iodessa Porphyra |
| **Commerce** | Entertainment, Occult oddities |
| **Deities** | [[Murex]], [[Orcein]] |

## Summary
A ritzy and aristocratic nation. The people mostly provide entertainment for those up north. They have a love for the exotic, particularly the taboo, and hold grand parties centered around various whatever happens to fancy them at the time. Methysto is a country of endless nights and non-stop partying.

## Culture
Methystans absolutely adore anything otherworldly or macabre. They are also very social and habitual party-goers. Though many outside might consider their tastes to be quite immoral, they care more for what’s fun. Their lavish lifestyles do have an effect on their ego, and it’s not uncommon for people to end up punished for small slights. Methystans find prisons drab and laborious, however, and instead raise those who commit crimes as undead thralls.

## Government
Though most nobles are autonomous, they tend to follow certain guidelines to stay in connection with other richer aristocracy. This goes double for any sort of social gatherings or celebrations. It’s more of a tacit agreement that those who want to maintain influence keep in favor of others. The reason being that who becomes the Royal Eminence is the one who can maintain favor and interest during the national Orchella Festival. The current Eminence, Iodessa Porphyra, has made quite the show of her influence since she won the last Orchella Festival, and has been a trendsetter in the performing arts.

## Environment
Likely due to arcane remnants of the Chromaclasm, the country is in an eternal midnight, with the sun’s rays being reflected by a violet aura. This allows the drow and other nocturnal beings to stay active during the day. The high affinity of magic in the area has affected the ecosystem as well, and the wild is full of magical beasts and plants, much to the Methystans’ pleasure.

## Settlements
### Fandango
The people of Fandango believe in creating carnivals for the general public. This town was founded by a group of wandering circus performers known as the Fuschia Troupe and moves from time to time.

### Heliotrope
Being so close to the Allium Grove, the people here are quite familiar with the odd beasts and magical creatures. Expect to see some very interesting “pets” going around the streets. The city is littered with iridescent crystals that seem to radiate with intense arcane energy.

### Lavender
Home of the previous Eminence, Petunia, the place has a more mercantile atmosphere. She was much more hedonistic and hoarding, and it reflects in her city of lust and splendor.

### Orchid
A land of picturesque beauty. They’re not as loud and rambunctious as the other towns, preferring quiet, thoughtful muses such as art, poetry, and sculpting. Works made there are worth several fortunes.

### Starling
A courier town. Her Eminence has transformed the area into a large opera house and theater where she goes to perform. Singers and actors flock here. They’ve also taken to making the couriers sing their messages, much to other countries’ chagrin.

### Wisteria (Wih-steer-ee-uh)
More of a giant palace than a city. This place is reserved for the Eminence and their royal court. They typically invite only the most prestigious of individuals to the area. This is where the Orchella Festival is held, where influential figures can compete with others for social status and a potential seat as the new Eminence.

## Other Places
### Allium Grove
A strange woodland filled with magical crystals. These crystals seem to be highly-concentrated arcane magic, and have had a significant effect on the local fauna and flora. Nearby towns will go hunting for these exotic creatures and bring them to interesting aristocrats.

### Lilac Strait
The large body of water bridging Wisteria with the rest of Methysto. The waters are lit up with party boats and wild magical spectacles.
