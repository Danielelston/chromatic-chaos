# Seppheros

*Pronounced:* Seffe-ur-ohs · *Demonym:* Sepph/Indigonous

The remains of a lost civilization in the north, it is now overrun with the corrupting magic of the Chromaclasm. This distortion has nearly wiped out or warped all life in the area. The only remains are the monsters that roam the lands of Akroma. It’s said that descendants of those who survived the Chromaclasm thousands of years ago live in seclusion within the dead country. They are known as the Indigonous for the belief that they are somehow tied to Indigo.
