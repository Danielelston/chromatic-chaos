# Argrenath — The Sanguine Hearth

*Pronounced:* arr-gruh-nath; Adj. Grent

| | |
|---|---|
| **Capital** | Crimson |
| **Races** | Dwarves, Goliaths, subterranean races |
| **Political** | The Red Barons |
| **Commerce** | Metalworks, Mining, Labor |
| **Deities** | [[Cochineal]], [[Kermes]], [[Kerr Lacca]] |

## Summary
It is a country of hard-working, battle-hardened people. It specializes in industry, particularly metalworking, and is very strict on the laws and governance. Visitors must even proceed through various customs to get in and out of the country. They pride themselves on being self-sufficient, however due to the country’s extreme climate and heavy refining, resources have become limited.

## Culture
The people of Argrenath are hard-working and close-knit. They don’t welcome those who would lie, steal, or devalue good craft. Honor is everything in Argrenath, and the highest crime one can commit is theft. Most offenders are usually thrown in The Rust, but some might just settle disputes with an ax through the skull. As per the teachings of Madder, family is of utmost importance to Grents. Though the clans are originally dwarvish, they’ve extended to many other races as time has gone on. Some clan heads have even been non-dwarfs.

## Government
Dwarven clans in Argrenath each specialize in a specific trade. The houses are not only responsible for parts of the commerce and production, but also elect head families to represent functions of government. These heads, called Barons, all meet in the capital to negotiate proceedings and the values of goods and services. There are 10 in all, each with a large stake in how their hold is shared among others. This is important since Grents don’t deal in currency, but offer bartering and favors from their clans. A favor from a Baron’s clan can be worth its weight in gold.

## Organizations
The Firesteel Ironworks and Mantle Mining, headed by the Firesmith and Mantlestone clans respectively, are the most influential groups in the country. The Garnet Guard is a huge extended family of warriors who protect the country.

## Environment
The land is hot, rocky, and arid due to volcanic activity. Some hardier creatures have grown accustomed to the hotter climate, such as the steelshell turtles. Much of the ground is solid and rocky like hardened magma, making it difficult to build or farm on. For this reason, the people of Argrenath built their cities primarily underground, and have raised various subterranean creatures for resources. Most notable are warthogs and giant moles. The surface is mostly used for smaller encampments and larger transports.

## Settlements
### Amaranth
A homelier city and the center of trade in Argrenath. Several large bazaars are set up for trade of goods and services across the country. There’s also many who come here to start up their own businesses. Many prospecting workers and artisans will flock here hoping to be scouted by one of the Barons.

### Burgundy
The only major city that is actually above ground. The runoff produced from the Carmine Rivers is high in minerals needed to sustain most wildlife, and as such it has been made the head of agriculture in Argrenath.

### Cardinal
The courier of Argrenath. It’s an outpost beyond the Cordovan mostly used to keep records of visitors. It also has temporary housing for the dwarven guards and visitors. It is above ground and sports several entry tunnels through the Blush Mountains. The teleportation circle is under heavy restrictions, and will not allow anyone in or out without a visa.

### Cordovan
Part of an impenetrable stone wall that spans across the northern border of Argrenath, this massive bastion stands as a checkpoint and the only means of entry or exit into the country by land. The Garnet Guard is headquartered here, ready to mobilize should war ever break out.

### Crimson
The capital of Argrenath, it lies deep inside Mt. Cinnabar. It is the steelworking hot spot, using the intense pressure produced by the volcano to create extra-hardened metals such as adamantine. Once a month, barons from across the country come to discuss trade and national issues at the House of Barons.

### Maroon
A port town situated inside of an open cavern in the southern peninsula. It serves as the center for fishing as well as a checkpoint for vessels. The lack of boating material in Argrenath has prevented them from exporting goods, so instead they rely on what they call crag-fishing. Some families have also been working on developing boats from other materials here.

### Sangria
A place for rough and rowdy drinkers. A popular locale for those looking for entertainment or a place to rest. It is also the only direct way of getting to Crimson. Sangria has a unique love for adventurers and offers fun and food to anyone willing to do an odd or dangerous job.

## Other Places
### Blush Mountains
A mountain range surrounding the east border. A large quantity of precious ores are in the mountains, and as such large tunnel systems snake throughout the entire range. As time went by, several towns and cities were developed within these mountains. The higher peaks are home to many Goliath settlements.

### Carmine Rivers
If Mt. Cinnabar is considered the heart of Argrenath, then Carmine Rivers would be its veins. Carrying nutrient-rich runoff from the volcanic waste, it is a veritable oasis from the otherwise infertile rocklands of the country.

### Mt. Cinnabar
The heart of Argrenath. It’s a large and mostly inactive volcano in the northwest of the country. The heat and pressure it produces makes it excellent for ironworking, and several forges were set up within it. It’s because of this, and its natural defenses from invasion, that it became the location of Argrenath’s capital.

### The Rust
An inescapable prison hosting dangerous criminals. Most who end up here spend the rest of their lives doing hard labor for the country. Since Blush Mountains hosts a wealth of precious ores, Mantle Mining has a facility situated here using the prisoners for labor.
