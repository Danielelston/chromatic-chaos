# Inyxo

*Pronounced:* In-nix-oh · *Demonym:* Nyxian

An island far to the west with an active volcano that clouds the sky in ashes. It’s said demons and their ilk were once exiled there. It’s currently surrounded in black mist and is said to be cursed by Soot. Some inhabitants will occasionally find their way to the mainland. They are typically demonic in appearance, sporting horns and even tails sometimes.

**Deity:** [[Soot]] (said to have cursed the island)
