# Adamos

*Pronounced:* Add-uh-mohs · *Demonym:* Adamosan

A country far east in the Bleakish Ocean that has been untouched by the Chromaclasm. It’s believed that the angels who fought in the Rebellion came from this country as agents of the Allkrom. It’s now home to the Church of the Allkrom, believed to be the architect of the world and its gods. The Church is held in Alabaster, and is led by Archciel Leukas Phthello. Some of the inhabitants, including Phthello, are angelic in appearance with glowing eyes and sometimes silvery marks along their body.

**Deity:** [[Allkrom]]
