# Courier Towns

After the War of the Blazing Sun, it was decided by Covalt that mediation and communication should be established between all countries to hopefully avoid future conflicts. This birthed the courier system. Couriers were Covalti birds magically given the ability to transport messages and small packages over large distances. Each town acted as a conduit for such magic, named after the country’s favored bird of choice: Cardinal, Oriole, Canary, Kakapo, Jay, Starling, and Dove. Recent developments in the magic behind couriers also brought the advancement of teleportation circles. However Flavo Topis and Argrenath have heavily restricted teleportation circles within their couriers, due to their experimental nature and the two countries’ disdain towards arcane magic. Due to maintenance and security, teleportation between nations is very expensive and typically reserved for noteworthy individuals. There’s also strict bans on teleportation outside of registered circles.
