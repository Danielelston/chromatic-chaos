# Mudruckers

A long-running movement of people who denounce their nation and creed to roam the world freely. They discard their names and take names and identities related to the color brown, representing the mix of all walks of life. They can be found anywhere, but favor Cirranbar and the Maradosan forests, which are open and free of judgment.
