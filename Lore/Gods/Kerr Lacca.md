# Kerr Lacca

**Goddess of fire, fury, and bloodshed**

*Pronounced:* Kair Lack-uh

Kerr Lacca is the embodiment of hardship and grief incarnated as fury and hatred. Those who follow her are the ones who wish to bathe in the blood of those who wronged them. Berserkers and barbarians alike make sacrifices and rituals of their kills in her name. Many mark themselves with tattoos representing griefs and struggles that fuel their fire.

- **Values:** Show no mercy to your enemies, let your hatred fly free, wear your scars like badges of honor
- **Temples:** Deep in Mt. Cinnabar’s magma chambers
- **Symbol:** An erupting volcano under a blood red sun
- **Manifestation:** A bright red phoenix wreathed in flames with a sun disc hovering her like a halo
- **PF2E Domains:** Destruction, Fire, Pain, Sorrow; Favored Weapon: Greatsword

**Nation:** [[Argrenath]]
