# Woad

**Goddess of the sky, wisdom, and seasons** (N)

Woad is said to embody the winds of change. She moved the skies and the waves so that man could further pursue the world around them. Woad instilled the virtues of curiosity and passion into mankind.

- **Values:** Pursue knowledge, share your wisdom with all, never fall complacent
- **Symbol:** A shooting star with a trail of wind behind it
- **PF2E Domains:** Air, Change, Knowledge, Magic; Favored Weapon: Staff Mordants ● Also named after natural dyes/pigments. ● Lesser gods though still revered. They kind of function like celestial beings that reside over mankind ● While all perform a role and are respected, most choose only one to worship. ● Each has at least one physical manifestation of itself.
