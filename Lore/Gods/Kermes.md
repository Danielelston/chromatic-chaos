# Kermes

**God of trade, forge, and community**

*Pronounced:* Kur-mees

Kermes is the founder of early civilization. He established a community through a system of bartering and trading. The current system of commerce in Argrenath is based on his teachings. It’s believed that the exchange of goods and services is the proper way to ensure that the value of the craft is never lost.

- **Values:** Provide for your community, always provide fair exchange of goods and services, never take anything for granted
- **Temples:** The House of Barons, Amaranth
- **Symbol:** A shield with a bristled pattern emblazoned on it
- **Manifestation:** A giant ironshell turtle with red brambles layering his shell
- **PF2E Domains:** Cities, Creation, Family, Wealth; Favored Weapon: Mace

**Nation:** [[Argrenath]]
