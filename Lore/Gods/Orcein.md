# Orcein

**Goddess of art, wine, and revelry**

*Pronounced:* Oar-see-enn

Orcein is only appeased by the fanciest and wildest of parties. Her influence over Methysto has led to its current ruling system and the biennial Orchella Festival to determine the new Eminence. Instead of a formal temple, Methystans simply leave patronage at the Royal Court by having overly extravagant parties in her name.

- **Values:** Never have a dull moment in life, live life like a party, never settle for anything that isn’t absolutely beautiful or ostentatious
- **Temples:** Wisteria, The Royal Palace of the Eminence
- **Symbol:** A painting of a spilled glass of wine with the wine leaking out of the portrait
- **Manifestation:** A giant violet peacock whose song carries for miles. She can transport through paintings
- **PF2E Domains:** Indulgence, Luck, Secrecy, Star; Favored Weapon: Whip

**Nation:** [[Methysto]]
