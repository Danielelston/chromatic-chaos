# Broom

**God of wealth, fortune, and trickery**

Broom respects all who respect the coin and are clever enough to obtain it. A bit of a trickster, his blessings tend to have unforeseen consequences. Though he’s not whimsical, he intends to teach his followers modesty. Broom knows that those who get too comfortable with their possessions will fall victim to complacency and greed.

- **Values:** Make sure you earn your keep, always be one step ahead, take stock of valuable assets
- **Temples:** The Fool’s Wisdom, Harlequin
- **Symbol:** A coin with a jester face on it
- **Manifestation:** A small bipedal toad wearing a jester cap and carrying a cane
- **PF2E Domains:** Ambition, Luck, Trickery, Wealth; Favored Weapon: Hand Crossbow

**Nation:** [[Maradosa]]
