# Cochineal

**God of war, glory, and strength**

*Pronounced:* Kotch-in-eel

Cochineal the Hammer is a ruthless and stalwart god. He believes that the only way to protect what’s yours is through hard-fought bloodshed. A much more militant god than the rest, many warriors and brutes will take up his domain during battle. Most weapons made in Argrenath are forged under his blessing.

- **Values:** Might makes right, actions speak louder than words, protect what’s yours
- **Temples:** Firestone Smithworks, Cordovan
- **Symbol:** A spartan helmet with a jewel in one eye hole
- **Manifestation:** An armored bull with hammers chained to ends of his bronze yoke
- **PF2E Domains:** Ambition, Cochineal, Might, Zeal; Favored Weapon: Warhammer

**Nation:** [[Argrenath]]
