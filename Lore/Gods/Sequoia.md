# Sequoia

**God of clay, birth, and beginnings**

*Pronounced:* Seh-koi-uh

Sequoia is typically worshipped in respect to birth and fertility. The major gods had raised Sequoia from clay and tasked it with sculpting creatures like itself around the world from the fertile soil below. A common phrase in Cirranbar is inspired by it: “If you’re looking to start anew, start from the ground up.”

- **Values:** All life has potential, there are no mistakes only hidden creations, respect the soul and body
- **Temples:** None
- **Symbol:** A clay mask with upside down facial features
- **Manifestation:** A multi-armed clay golem with sculpting tools in each hand.
- **PF2E Domains:** Change, Creation, Earth, Soul; Favored: Weapon: Shortsword
