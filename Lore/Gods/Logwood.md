# Logwood

**God of history, fates, and the stars**

Logwood the Starseer can chart the stars to see both the physical locations and the destinations of fates themselves. The Logwood Library is a temple full of his teachings and readings of both the past and future. Many go there in search of knowledge and guidance.

- **Values:** It’s folly to ignore wisdom , never forget the past, find the truth in all signs.
- **Temples:** Logwood Library, Mt. Celeste
- **Symbol:** a Greek torch with glittering blue flames
- **Manifestation:** A pegasus with glittering stars adorning his wing that trail his path
- **PF2E Domains:** Fate, Glyph, Knowledge, Star; Favored Weapon: Starknife
