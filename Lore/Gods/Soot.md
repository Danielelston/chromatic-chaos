# Soot

**God of darkness, finality, and ash**

Soot is an old and forgotten god, no longer worshiped so much as feared. Unlike Catechu who waits for ends, Soot acts to snuff out life without prejudice. It's believed that only through death and destruction can life begin anew. His followers seek to cleanse and purge themselves of all before them in order to be reborn through the ashes. Many are quite fanatical about fire and destruction.

- **Values:** Birth though destruction, sentiment is weakness, ashes to ashes, dust to dust
- **Temples:** Somewhere deep in the Spine of Ashes in Inyxo
- **Symbol:** A black ring of fire and smoke
- **Manifestation:** ???
- **PF2E Domains:** Darkness, Death, Destruction, Plague; Favored Weapon: Greataxe
