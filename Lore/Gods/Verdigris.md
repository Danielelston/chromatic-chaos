# Verdigris

**Goddess of nature, foraging, and medicine**

*Pronounced:* Vair-dih-gree

Verdigris resides over the forests of Maradosa but extends her blessing to all who accept the wild as their home. Those who live away from civilization or seek nature’s blessing take heed of her teachings. She is known to hail down and enact her wrath on those who would disrespect her domain or take more than what is bestowed.

- **Values:** Respect nature, live free of urban comforts, take only what’s needed to survive
- **Temples:** The Great Oak, Laurel
- **Symbol:** An elk head with tree branches for antlers
- **Manifestation:** A large elk with tree branches for antlers
- **PF2E Domains:** Healing, Nature, Swarm, Water; Favored Weapon: Longbow

**Nation:** [[Maradosa]]
