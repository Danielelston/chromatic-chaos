# Quercitron

**God of harvest, feasts, and livestock**

*Pronounced:* Kair-sit-rohn

Quercitron oversees the croplands of all farmers. Many farmers take heed of his guidance to maintain healthy produce. Those who would sell bad or overpriced food would seek his wrath and find their lands to be withered and infertile on the coming harvest.

- **Values:** Care for your animals, respect the land which you harvest, take integrity in what you grow and sell.
- **Temples:** The Cornucopia, Maize
- **Symbol:** A cornucopia full of wheat and barley
- **Manifestation:** A collection of small, chubby, ankylosaur-like creatures. Each one carries a pouch of seeds on its back.
- **PF2E Domains:** Decay, Indulgence, Moon, Swarm; Favored Weapon: Scythe
