# Saffron

**Goddess of freedom, journeys, and the horizon**

Also known as One-Eyed June, Saffron is believed to have reached godhood after journeying west to a lost promised land. Her nickname comes from the misconception that she was lost or that the place she sought did not exist.

- **Values:** Follow your dreams, chase the horizon, express who you are
- **Temples:** No formal temple. Her followers are never tied down to one place.
- **Symbol:** A sunrise peeking over the horizon
- **Manifestation:** A desert fox with a rising sun on her back and doesn’t leave pawprints where she treads
- **PF2E Domains:** Air, Freedom, Sun, Travel; Favored Weapon: Fist

**Nation:** [[Cirranbar]]
