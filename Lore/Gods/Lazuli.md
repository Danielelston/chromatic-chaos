# Lazuli

**Goddess of creativity, dreams, and planes**

*Pronounced:* Lah-zuh-lee

Lazuli the Dreamweaver visits the minds of people during their sleep to give them inspiration & respite. She dwells in an unknown plane that connects to the subconscious of those in the material plane. It’s said that the song of a nightingale is Lazuli giving her blessing during your dreams.

- **Values:** Imagine beyond the fathomable, find inspiration in every corner, there are no accidents, only cause for creativity
- **Temples:** The Dreamer’s Dens scattered throughout Covalt
- **Symbol:** A nightingale perched on clouds and larkspur
- **Manifestation:** A flock of nightingales that flow from a deep blue cloud
- **PF2E Domains:** Delirium, Dreams, Nightmares, Repose; Favored Weapon: Glaive

**Nation:** [[Covalt]]
