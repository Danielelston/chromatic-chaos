# Fustic

**God of rulership, polis, and law**

*Pronounced:* Fyoo-stick

Fustic is the enforcer of Weld’s edicts. He is more vocal than most gods and is responsible for selecting the new king of Flavo Topis. His role is to maintain order and peace throughout all cities through strict ordinances. Those who oppose these laws will face harsh punishment.

- **Values:** Respect all authority, enforce the law to each fellow man, never turn a blind eye to the unlawful
- **Temples:** The Hall of Edicts, Castle Fulvos.
- **Symbol:** A crossed sword and aspergillum with an eye overlaid at the intersection
- **Manifestation:** Twin hounds wearing judge wigs, one carries a sword and the other carries an aspergillum
- **PF2E Domains:** Cities, Duty, Truth, Tyranny; Favored Weapon: Longsword

**Nation:** [[Flavo Topis]]
