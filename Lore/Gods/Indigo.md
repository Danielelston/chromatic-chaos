# Indigo

**God of the night, magic, and monsters**

Very little is known about this god; they’re identity, history, nor even appearance. The name “Indigo” was only given to them by historians based on ancient texts. Old worshippers seem to be all but forgotten and new acolytes are scattered and secretive. It does appear, however, that patronage of this mordant is not through worship, but through the choosing of a very intimate ritual that is known only by a handful of people. Though they’re identity is a mystery, their presence is well known throughout Akroma through the terrible beasts and horrors that roam the night.

- **Values:** Become one with the night, there is beauty in the monstrous, guard secrets with conviction
