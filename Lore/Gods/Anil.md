# Anil

**Goddess of the sea, sailing, and storms**

*Pronounced:* Ah-neel

Anil the Pirate Queen rules both the sea and sails. It is always important that any vessel pay tithe to the ocean before setting sail, lest they earn her wrath. She is fierce and quick-tempered, and the sea is littered with wrecked ships that have not followed her law. It’s tradition for any pirate to throw part of their haul overboard before sinking a vessel, called Anil’s Due.

- **Values:** Take what you desire, give back to the water for safe travel, never stay anchored to one place.
- **Temples:** The Shipyard, Zaffre Isles
- **Symbol:** A scimitar with waves along the blade
- **Manifestation:** A kraken wearing a wrecked ship like a pirate hat, and wielding 8 scimitars
- **PF2E Domains:** Air, Cold, Lightning, Travel; Favored Weapon: Scimitar
