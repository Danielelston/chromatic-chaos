# Catechu

**Goddess of death, decay, and disease**

*Pronounced:* Cat-eh-shoo

Catechu (or Lady Cutch) serves as the grim reaper of sorts, waiting at the end of every lifeline. She is known to destroy everything she touches and seeks the end of all civilizations so that something stronger may take its place.

- **Values:** Destroy the weak and incapable, serve your life up to that which is greater, leave nothing dead to waste.
- **Temples:** None
- **Symbol:** A beetle with a skull on its back
- **Manifestation:** Swarms of carrion beetles with a skull on their backs.
- **PF2E Domains:** Death, Decay, Dust, Fate; Favored Weapon: Dagger
