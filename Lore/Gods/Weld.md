# Weld

**God of the sun, purity, and justice** (LG)

Weld is said to have carried the sun by a chain like a flail. He used his light to nourish the living and guide the people to prosperity. His light also purges the evils that would dare threaten his gifts. Weld instilled the virtues of valor and altruism into mankind.

- **Values:** Vanquish evil, protect the needy, honor our sun
- **Symbol:** A flail with the sun as the head
- **PF2E Domains:** Confidence, Fire, Sun, Zeal; Favored Weapon: Flail
