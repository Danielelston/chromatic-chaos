# Gamboge

**God of the sands, hunting, and the wild**

*Pronounced:* Gam-bohj

Gamboge teaches humbleness and strength through hardships and sacrifice. He is said to roam the dunelands of Cirranbar and hunt down the weak. In his domain, you are either the predator, or the prey.

- **Values:** Only the strong survive, trials and tribulations make you who you are, it's kill or be killed
- **Temples:** Small shrines in bandit sects, including Lion’s Den.
- **Symbol:** A cobra coiled around an hourglass
- **Manifestation:** A giant desert cobra with an hourglass on its hood and a scorpion stinger on his tail.
- **PF2E Domains:** Dust, Might, Nature, Time; Favored Weapon: Spear

**Nation:** [[Cirranbar]]
