# Myrabalan

**Goddess of redemption, love, and healing**

*Pronounced:* Mee-ruh-bae-lan

Myrabalan believes there is good in all of mankind, whether they express it or not. Many come to her domain for aid in atoning for their sins or forgiving the sins of others. It’s believed that she wards the spirits of those who cannot move on.

- **Values:** Forgive and heal, strive to do good by those around you, love who you are
- **Temples:** House of Healing, Flaxen
- **Symbol:** Two hands embracing a heart
- **Manifestation:** A swarm of golden butterflies that sound like church bells as they fly
- **PF2E Domains:** Healing, Passion, Protection, Repose; Favored Weapon: Rapier

**Nation:** [[Flavo Topis]]
