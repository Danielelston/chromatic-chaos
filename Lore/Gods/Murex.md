# Murex

**God of lust, murder, and revenge**

*Pronounced:* Myur-eks

Murex is a bitter and obsessive god. He and his followers are never to be trifled with. Though quick-tempered, he is artful in his ways of dealing out punishment, seeking to make impressive displays of his victims. His favored method of killing, which is often mimicked by his followers, is exsanguination followed by using the victim’s blood to paint works of art. Murex prefers his revenge to be excruciatingly painful and drawn out.

- **Values:** Never let anyone slight you, make your kills a works of art, a follower’s work is seen but never heard
- **Temples:** The House of Pain, Lavender
- **Symbol:** A murex shell
- **Manifestation:** A tiny sea snail with spines full of various toxins and elixirs. He can appear in any shell
- **PF2E Domains:** Cold, Death, Pain, Trickery; Favored Weapon: Spiked Chain
