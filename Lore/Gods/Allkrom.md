# Allkrom

The people of Adamos claim him to be a being with a more direct influence than the other Mordants. Either some higher deity, an ancient being, or an entity from beyond, his true identity is only known to the Archciel (basically the pope), and they are the only one who hears his words.

- **PF2E Domains:** Perfection, Time, Vigil, Void; Favored Weapon: Morningstar
