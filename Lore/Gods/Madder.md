# Madder

**God of the earth, honor, and family** (LN)

Madder is said to be the one who built Akroma’s natural borders to keep the people from straying too far from their homelands. He forged and sculpted the lands from using the volcanoes. Madder instilled the virtues of loyalty and integrity into mankind.

- **Values:** Defend your name, care for your home and family, take pride in your lineage
- **Symbol:** A mountain with a fist emblazoned on it.
- **PF2E Domains:** Duty, Earth, Family, Protection; Favored Weapon: Pick
