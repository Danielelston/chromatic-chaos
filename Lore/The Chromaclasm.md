# The Chromaclasm

Millennia ago, the world of Akroma was very different. There were a myriad of powerful and ancient races and creatures, not least of all were the dragons. They freely roamed the land with their awesome power. However, that all changed when a magical explosion of untold proportions warped the land, destroying the people, their civilization, and the dragons. Not much is known about the cause of such an event, but it has forever changed the world of Akroma, and its terrible corruption influences it to this very day.
