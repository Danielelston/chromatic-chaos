# Campaign Pitch

## Chromatic Chaos
*"Akroma is a world painted in every shade imaginable. Nine nations each carry their own color, and with it their own way of life. There’s red Argrenath, forged in fire and iron. There’s green Maradosa, where you’ll need three forms and a stamp just to sneeze. And there’s violet Methysto, where elections are decided by talent show. Gods named after dyes walk the land, from harvest spirits who bless the crops to a pirate queen who demands her share of every haul.*

*You are registered adventurers, which in Maradosa means you’ve survived the paperwork. Your newest contract looks simple: travel to the ruins of Seppheros, the most dangerous place in Akroma, and bring back a relic for a client who pays very well and asks very few questions.*

*What could possibly go wrong?"*

This is a lighthearted adventure full of goofy sidequests, quirky villains, and strange local customs. You'll journey across all nine nations, each with its own flavor, problems, and trouble to get into. Your characters start at level 3, as adventurers who've already seen a bit of the world. Bring a rough idea of who your character is. We'll work out the details, including backstory and origin, at session 0, and be sure to leave room open to discover more about them as we play.
