# Gods & Mordants

- Named after natural dyes/pigments — things that create colors.
- **The Gods** are major beings worshipped by all. Temples to them are found in about every major city. They don't have physical forms.
- **Mordants** are lesser gods, still revered. They function like celestial beings that reside over mankind. All perform a role and are respected, but most choose to worship only one. Each has at least one physical manifestation.

## Maître Wellwood: Protect the past, present, and future
Not really a god so much as an ideology. It is the combined faiths of the 3 major gods, Madder, Weld, and Woad. [[Madder]] upholds one's heritage and history, [[Weld]] upholds one's people and ruler, and [[Woad]] upholds the knowledge to be passed to the next generation.
